* January 2019 - March 2023: **PhD student**
  * Stellantis, UTC, UNIGE, VELIZY – FRANCE
  This PhD was on the topic of AI safety for autonomous vehicles. It was done in partnership with the international car manufacturer Stellantis. We focused on neural network based AI for the decision of autonomous vehicles. Our work contributed to improving the safety assessment framework for AI-based autonomous driving systems, leading to two international publications.

  * Tasks and experiments:
    - state of the art of the existing NN safety approaches
    - development and tuning of a neural network performing an autonomous driving task
    - formal verification of a safety property on the neural network
    - fault injection on a neural network to be formally verified
    - design of driving test scenarios to verify the dependability of the neural network via testing in a driving simulator

  * Main scientific contributions:
    - proposed a characterization of neural networks from a software development perspective
    -identified a new threat to formal verification for neural network
    - developed a concrete example of formal verification on an erroneous model of neural network.
    - proposed a fault tolerance mechanisms (detection & recovery) for the new threat
    - developed a set of processes and feedback tools to ease and enable the implementation of formal verification for neural network
