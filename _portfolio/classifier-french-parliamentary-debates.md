This project uses Natural Language Processing techniques to classify speeches from the French National Assembly. The objective is to predict the political group of a deputy solely based on the content and style of their interventions.

The dataset is composed of transcripts from public parliamentary debates. I used pre-trained transformer models (CamemBERT) and fine-tuned them for multi-class text classification. 

Key aspects:
- Data preprocessing and formatting for model input
- Fine-tuning of a CamemBERT model using HuggingFace Transformers
- Evaluation of model accuracy and analysis of classification biases
- Visualization of speech embeddings for exploratory analysis

This project is part of my effort to build strong hands-on experience with LLMs and demonstrate my ability to work with real-world language data in a political and social context.
