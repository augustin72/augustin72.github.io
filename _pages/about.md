Welcome to my personal page! I'm Augustin, a computer science engineer with a background in AI and machine learning. I hold degrees in computer engineering from [UTC](https://utc.fr) and [UNIGE](https://unige.it) and completed a Ph.D. focusing on AI safety for autonomous vehicles. This PhD was done in collaboration with the car manufacturer [Stellantis](https://www.stellantis.com). I graduated in March 2023 from [Sorbonne University](https://www.sorbonne-universite.fr) and [UNIGE](https://unige.it). My work includes publications in international journals on AI safety.

Currently, I'm expanding my expertise in large language models (LLM), actively learning about their capabilities and applications. I have experience in Python and PyTorch, and I am eager to work on projects that apply AI to solve real-world challenges. I’m passionate about building and fine-tuning models to improve performance and reliability, particularly in AI-driven environments.

Feel free to explore my projects, and if you’re looking for a dedicated and adaptive engineer, I would love to connect!
